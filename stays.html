Independent Stays

Photos for places with soul.

I photograph places I'd love to stay in myself: Airbnbs with character, surf hostels, family-run hotels. No sterile real-estate shots — photos that show what it feels like to stay with you. No deposit, no hidden costs, no fine print.

How it works

01

Send me a short message about your place and what you need.

02

We find a date.

03

I come by and shoot while you carry on as usual.

04

Within 5 business days, you get a link with all final photos. You pay after delivery.

Stay & Shoot

No budget, but empty beds? Let's trade.

You host me for at least 3 nights, I photograph your place — 10–12 final photos you can use for your marketing. Available only in places my travels take me anyway, so just ask if your region fits right now.

Fair for both

  • We put the essentials in writing beforehand — short and simple, one page.
  • I get to use the photos for my portfolio and channels.
  • When you publish the photos, you credit me as the photographer.

Good to know

  • All prices are final — no VAT is added (German small business regulation, §19 UStG).
  • Travel within Berlin & surroundings included. Further away? Tell me where and I'll make you a fair offer.
  • Need the photos faster than 5 business days? Mention it — usually something's possible.
